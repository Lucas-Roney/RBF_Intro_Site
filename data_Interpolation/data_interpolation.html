Tidal Data Interpolation

About Tidal Interpolation

This tool demonstrates how Radial Basis Functions can be used to interpolate missing sections of real-world tidal data. You can cut out a portion of the dataset and use RBF interpolation to reconstruct the missing values.

  • Load Data: The tidal dataset loads automatically.
  • Cut Tools: Remove a section manually or by selecting a region.
  • Interpolation: Choose a kernel and ε to fill the missing gap.
  • Best ε: Automatically finds the ideal shape parameter.
